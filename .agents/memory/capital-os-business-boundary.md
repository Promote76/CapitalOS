---
name: Business capital boundary
description: Rules for keeping operating-company books separate from household capital and net worth.
---

Business revenue and profit remain on business books. Owner contributions and intercompany transfers are not revenue; owner distributions are not operating expenses. Business cash never becomes household Safe-to-Deploy directly. Only a completed, human-reviewed distribution may become household income.

**Why:** Combining operating cash, profit, distributions, and household cash would overstate deployable capital and could double count business value in family net worth.

**How to apply:** Calculate business performance and Safe-to-Distribute independently in integer cents, enforce business reserve floors before creating a distribution review, and include either ownership-adjusted business equity or its underlying assets in family net worth—not both.
