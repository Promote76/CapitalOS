---
name: Property underwriting guardrails
description: Durable boundaries for the Capital OS acquisition-planning experience.
---

Property acquisition planning must keep household readiness, affordability, financing estimates, and property-level deal quality as separate signals. The Property Capital Governor is a hard safety boundary; advisory analysis cannot override liquidity floors or protected-capital rules.

**Why:** A property can look attractive while the household is not ready to acquire it, and combining those signals makes an optimistic score look like approval.

**How to apply:** Keep every future property surface labeled as an estimate or planning signal, preserve provider-neutral/manual-first inputs, and route any capital-use decision through the governor.
