---
name: Property underwriting guardrails
description: Durable boundaries for the Capital OS acquisition-planning experience.
---

Property acquisition planning must keep household readiness, affordability, financing estimates, and property-level deal quality as separate signals. The Property Capital Governor is a hard safety boundary; advisory analysis cannot override liquidity floors or protected-capital rules.

**Why:** A property can look attractive while the household is not ready to acquire it, and combining those signals makes an optimistic score look like approval.

**How to apply:** Keep every future property surface labeled as an estimate or planning signal, preserve provider-neutral/manual-first inputs, and route any capital-use decision through the governor.

Florida tax-lien research follows the same boundary: a candidate is blocked until official identity, redemption status, live availability, source freshness, property value, and reserve capacity are explicit; a clean assessment still requires human review and never authorizes a bid or purchase.

**Why:** Tax-lien records can look actionable while parcel identity, redemption rights, auction availability, and household reserve impact remain unresolved. Treating research completeness as approval would bypass the property and capital governors.

**How to apply:** Persist source and reconciliation state separately from deterministic underwriting, surface each hard stop, and keep tax-lien records out of purchase, bidding, deed, lender, OMS, Treasury, and household-capital paths.
