---
name: Research opportunity screen
description: Durable boundary for the Research Top 25 discovery workflow.
---

The Research Top 25 may combine current human-approved Schwab market observations, approved SEC evidence, and observed portfolio context only as an advisory ranking input. Pending, blocked, stale, unknown, or unreviewed evidence must be excluded; duplicate evidence for a ticker must collapse into one candidate. Portfolio observations may explain fit and concentration, but must not become balances, allocations, Safe-to-Deploy authority, or execution state. Provider refreshes may only stage pending drafts; discovery must not auto-approve them.

**Why:** The Research page needs to be useful without widening the existing read-only Schwab boundary or allowing a recommendation surface to become an order path.

**How to apply:** Keep discovery responses server-computed, household-scoped, provenance-preserving, freshness-gated, and explicit about manual-only actions. When the provider has no broad screener, use a bounded household-known symbol universe and report that coverage limit instead of implying market-wide discovery. Any future watch, shadow, journal, monitoring, or Schwab-link mutation must remain a separate advisory record and must not call OMS, transfers, or money movement.
