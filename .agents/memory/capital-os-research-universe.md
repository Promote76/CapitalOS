---
name: Verified-domestic research universes
description: Domestic classification, selection, batching, cursor, and authority rules for U.S. investment discovery.
---

Use a frozen, attributed SEC exchange-directory snapshot only after each issuer is classified from its official SEC submissions jurisdiction. U.S. states, DC, and U.S. territories are verified domestic; foreign and unknown jurisdictions are excluded rather than inferred from exchange. Confirm instrument type from an authoritative security-level directory, keep its source hash and evidence on each classification, and treat unmatched types as unknown rather than applying ticker heuristics. Universe selection is independent of the Income / Compounders / Balanced ranking lens. Process deterministic bounded batches only. Schwab remains a symbol-targeted, read-only enrichment source and must never be described as supplying or screening the universe.

**Why:** A U.S. exchange listing does not prove domestic domicile, and issuer metadata cannot reliably distinguish common shares, funds, preferred classes, warrants, and bundled units. Ticker suffix heuristics can silently misclassify those instruments. The authorized Schwab capabilities also do not include a provider-wide screener or practical symbol enumeration.

**How to apply:** Keep the SEC jurisdiction gate unchanged. Static type universes use frozen security-level evidence; unknown, warrant, unit, and right types never enter fund, preferred, or common-stock selections. Income, growth, and cap universes use approved/current household evidence; cap buckets derive USD value from approved shares outstanding times approved price and fail closed on missing inputs. Keep new observations pending review. Bind cursors to source version, universe, and custom allowlist; reset on any identity change.
