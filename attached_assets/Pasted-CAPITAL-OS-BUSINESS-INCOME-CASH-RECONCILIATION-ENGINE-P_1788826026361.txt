4. NO DOUBLE COUNTING

Critical invariant:

A Stevens settlement and the corresponding Wells Fargo deposit are
NOT two separate income events.

Example:

Stevens gross revenue:
$5,934.88

Stevens deductions:
$3,810.60

Stevens net settlement:
$2,124.28

Wells Fargo deposit:
$2,124.28

Correct interpretation:

Business revenue:
$5,934.88

Carrier settlement cash:
$2,124.28

Bank deposit:
same cash event

Household income:
NOT YET DETERMINED

Never calculate:

$5,934.88 + $2,124.28

as total income.
