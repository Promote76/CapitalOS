CAPITAL OS — CONNECT WELLS FARGO THROUGH PLAID

Continue development of the existing Capital OS application.

Add secure read-only Wells Fargo banking integration using Plaid.

Do not redesign the existing approved Capital OS interface.

The objective is to allow me to connect my personal Wells Fargo accounts to Capital OS so the Household Finance, Budget, Cash Flow, Duplex Goal, and Capital Governor modules can use real financial data.

CRITICAL SECURITY RULE

Capital OS must NEVER ask me to enter my Wells Fargo username or password directly into Capital OS or Replit.

Use Plaid Link and Wells Fargo's supported OAuth authorization flow.

Authentication and account authorization must happen through the official Plaid/Wells Fargo authorization experience.

Capital OS should only receive authorized financial data.

Never expose Plaid secrets or access tokens to the browser.

PHASE 1 ACCESS

The Wells Fargo connection must be READ ONLY.

Enable:

- Account discovery
- Current balances
- Available balances when provided
- Transaction history
- Account type/subtype
- Institution information
- Transaction synchronization

Do NOT enable:

- ACH transfers
- Wire transfers
- Bank-to-bank transfers
- Withdrawals
- Payment initiation
- Automated movement into Capital OS
- Automated movement into the Duplex Reserve
- Automated strategy funding

Capital OS cannot move money through Wells Fargo during this phase.

PLAID PRODUCTS

- Transactions
- Balance

Design the architecture so additional products such as Investments or Liabilities can be added later without rebuilding the integration.
