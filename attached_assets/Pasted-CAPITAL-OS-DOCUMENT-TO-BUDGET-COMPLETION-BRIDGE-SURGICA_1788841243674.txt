1. CURRENT DEFECT
   ==================================================

Current uploaded statement rows exist as:

BANK-STATEMENT EVIDENCE

Official Budget and Accounting use:

FINANCE TRANSACTIONS

There is currently no controlled bridge between them.

The system therefore cannot:

- assign uploaded rows to Budget categories;
- populate category actuals;
- match evidence to existing official transactions;
- import reviewed evidence as official household activity;
- update Accounting from approved imported statement rows;
- update forecasts from approved imported activity;
- refresh Capital Governor from those official transactions.

Fix that gap.
