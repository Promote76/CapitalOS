4. INCOME FLOOR

Create deterministic:

HouseholdIncomeFloor

The purpose is NOT to predict the maximum income.

The purpose is:

"How much recurring household spending can reasonably be supported
without assuming a strong income month?"

Do not hard-code one statistical method forever.

Create configurable policies such as:

CONSERVATIVE_MINIMUM

TRAILING_PERCENTILE

TRAILING_MEDIAN_DISCOUNTED

USER_APPROVED_FLOOR

Until sufficient history exists:

use:

INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY

or:

USER_APPROVED_FLOOR.

Never fabricate a statistically supported floor from inadequate data.
