This phase builds:

CAPITAL OS FAMILY OPERATIONS & AUTOMATION ENGINE

The purpose is to create a reliable operating cadence for the household.

Capital OS should not merely store financial information.

It should help ensure that:

- bills are not forgotten
- goals are reviewed
- duplex contributions remain on track
- property tasks are followed up
- stale banking data is refreshed
- approvals are not lost
- strategy reviews occur on schedule
- accounting closes happen
- security reviews occur
- reports are generated
- significant financial changes surface at the right time

Automation should reduce administrative burden without taking unauthorized financial action.
