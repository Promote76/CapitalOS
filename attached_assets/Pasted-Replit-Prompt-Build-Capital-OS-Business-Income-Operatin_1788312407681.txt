Capital OS should not merely track income.

It should identify:

Where income originates
How reliable it is
How profitable each business is
How much capital must remain inside the business
How much can safely reach the household
Where that household capital should go next

Maintain this hierarchy:

Business Survival
→ Household Stability
→ Protected Goals
→ Liquidity
→ Productive Reinvestment
→ Controlled Distributions
→ Wealth Compounding

Build accordingly.
