CAPITAL OS FAMILY ACCOUNTING & REPORTING ENGINE

The objective is to create an authoritative financial record of the household's:

- Assets
- Liabilities
- Income
- Expenses
- Savings
- Capital contributions
- Investment performance
- Real estate
- Treasury
- Strategy capital
- Property equity
- Net worth
- Realized gains/losses
- Unrealized gains/losses
- Tax lots
- Capital flows

Capital OS should be able to answer:

«What is the household worth today?»

«How did net worth change this month?»

«Where did the increase or decrease come from?»

«How much capital has been contributed versus earned?»

«What income did each asset produce?»

«What strategies actually added value?»

«How much equity exists in real estate?»

«What transactions may have tax consequences?»

«What information should be provided to an accountant?»

This module is for internal accounting, recordkeeping, analysis, and tax preparation support.

Capital OS must NOT present itself as a CPA, tax preparer, attorney, or official tax filing service.
