Continue building the existing Capital OS application.

Do not redesign the approved UI.

Preserve all existing modules, including:

- Household Budget
- Cash Flow

This phase adds the coordinated intelligence layer:

CAPITAL OS INTELLIGENCE

The purpose is to transform Capital OS from a passive financial dashboard into an intelligent family-capital advisory system.

The system should continuously help answer:

- What should the household prioritize next?
- Are we still on track for the duplex?
- Is spending affecting the acquisition timeline?
- How much capital is truly safe to deploy?
- Which investment strategies deserve attention?
- Which strategies should receive less capital?
- Which property candidate is strongest?
- What risks are increasing?
- What changed since the last review?
- What action would have the greatest positive impact on family net worth?

AI must remain ADVISORY ONLY.

AI must never have direct authority to:

- move money
- initiate bank transfers
- submit trades
- withdraw assets
- approve mortgages
- submit property offers
- change protected capital
- disable hard risk rules
- deploy smart contracts
- modify credentials
