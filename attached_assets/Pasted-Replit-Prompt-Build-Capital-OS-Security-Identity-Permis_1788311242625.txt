1. CORE SECURITY PRINCIPLE

Assume eventually:

- passwords may be compromised
- a browser session may be stolen
- an API key may leak
- a dependency may fail
- a user may click a malicious link
- a service may become unavailable
- a database may become corrupted
- an administrator may make an error
- AI may receive malicious external content
- a venue may disagree with Capital OS state

Therefore:

«No single compromised credential, session, service, or AI component should provide unrestricted authority over household capital.»
