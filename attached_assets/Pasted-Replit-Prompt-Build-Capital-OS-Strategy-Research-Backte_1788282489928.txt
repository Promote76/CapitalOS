CAPITAL OS STRATEGY LAB

The purpose is to create a controlled research environment where investment strategies must prove themselves before receiving real capital.

No real trading is allowed in this phase.

The Strategy Lab must support:

Research
↓
Historical Backtest
↓
Walk-Forward Validation
↓
Live Shadow Mode
↓
Paper Trading
↓
Micro-Live Eligibility Review

The goal is not to manufacture impressive backtests.

The goal is to determine:

«Does this strategy appear to have a repeatable positive edge after realistic execution costs, adverse selection, latency, fees, and risk?»

---

1. CORE PRINCIPLE

No strategy may receive live capital merely because:

- an AI created it
- a backtest looks profitable
- it performed well for a few days
- another trader claims it works
- a social media post recommends it

Capital OS must require evidence.

The default strategy status is:

UNPROVEN

A strategy becomes eligible for capital only after passing the Strategy Graduation Engine.
