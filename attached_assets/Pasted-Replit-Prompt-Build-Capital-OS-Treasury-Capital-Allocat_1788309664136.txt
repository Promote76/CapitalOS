This phase builds:

CAPITAL OS TREASURY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION ENGINE

The purpose is to create a disciplined central treasury that governs how household capital is divided between:

- Required household liquidity
- Emergency reserves
- Protected duplex capital
- Closing-cost reserves
- Opportunity reserves
- Treasury / low-risk cash management
- Strategy capital
- Property capital
- Future wealth-building goals

The Treasury Engine must answer:

«Where should the next dollar go?»

«How much cash must remain protected?»

«How much can safely be invested?»

«Are we overfunding or underfunding any capital bucket?»

«Can a strategy receive additional capital without hurting the duplex plan?»

«How much liquidity would remain after a property purchase?»

«When should investment capital be reduced and returned to reserves?»

The system must prioritize capital survival and goal completion over maximizing investment deployment.
