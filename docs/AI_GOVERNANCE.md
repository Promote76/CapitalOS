# AI Governance

AI recommendations are advisory only. AI may summarize household finance patterns, flag anomalies, identify recurring expenses, and suggest a review.

AI cannot:

- approve a transaction;
- change a protected goal;
- move household or internal capital;
- connect a bank;
- pay a bill; or
- submit a blockchain transaction.

Human approval is recorded as a decision and audit event. Approval changes the household’s documented intent; it does not create an external payment or autonomous money movement.
