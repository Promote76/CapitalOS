# Business Income Source Authority

Current business calculations may use only current parser generations and canonical, review-approved business records.

The authority chain is:

`canonical settlement evidence → business earnings reconciliation → business reserve protection → human-approved owner draw → VerifiedHouseholdIncomeEvent → variable-income planning → Budget`

P&L net income establishes business performance and reconciliation evidence. It is not household income. A settlement deposit also does not create household income or an owner draw. Owner draw approval remains a human permission boundary.

If a source correction or duplicate review is unresolved, downstream readiness stays blocked or review-gated. No money moves, bank writes, ACH, brokerage execution, or Micro-Live activation occurs in this workflow.
