# Capital Bucket Model

Capital OS distinguishes physical cash from its economic designation. Household operating cash, emergency reserve, vehicle reserve, annual-obligation reserve, Capital OS reserve, opportunity reserve, investment capital, Duplex Reserve, business buckets, and unclassified cash are never silently interchangeable.

Each bucket carries:

- current and target amounts;
- protected and liquid flags;
- physical account provenance where available;
- economic designation provenance;
- withdrawal or lock policy.

Business operating, tax, maintenance, escrow, advances, reimbursements, and pending business cash remain business evidence until an approved owner draw creates a verified household income event.

The Duplex Reserve is a protected bucket. Its designation cannot be used to cover household shortfalls, vehicles, business expenses, investing, Strategy Lab, or Micro-Live.
