# Capital Waterfall Policy

The default advisory order is:

1. Emergency Reserve
2. Vehicle Reserve
3. Annual Obligation Reserve
4. Capital OS Reserve
5. Opportunity Reserve
6. Investment Capital

The waterfall is deterministic, exact-cent, policy-versioned, and recommended only. Floor, base, and strong income scenarios are shown separately:

- floor protects continuity and does not authorize discretionary deployment;
- base funds required gaps before balanced surplus recommendations;
- strong allocates only after all required protection is satisfied.

No recommendation authorizes movement of physical cash.
