# Duplex Capital Policy

Duplex Reserve is a protected capital goal. Its balance, target, provenance, and lock are shown separately from Safe-to-Deploy and household capital surplus.

Duplex Reserve cannot fund:

- household shortfalls or routine spending;
- vehicle acquisition or ownership costs;
- business operations, tax, maintenance, escrow, or reimbursements;
- Strategy Lab, investment capital, or Micro-Live;
- any money movement without an explicitly approved future policy.

Property underwriting may use the protected reserve as an advisory readiness input, but research, readiness, and deal quality do not create authority to spend it.
