# Household Obligation Model

Mandatory obligations come from approved planning-period fixed/debt categories
and essential active bills. Essential variable costs come from approved
`variable_essential` planning categories. Required upcoming expenses are
calendar evidence for 7-, 14-, 30-, 60-, and 90-day windows.

Transfers are not spending. Business-linked accounts are not household cash.
Unreviewed transactions are not allowed to change the model. Credit-card
payment double counting remains governed by the existing transaction review and
transfer-classification rules rather than by adding raw payment rows again.
