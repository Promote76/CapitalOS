# Household Reserve Policy

The reserve policy is intentionally separate from the household cash-buffer policy.

- **Operating buffer:** near-term household continuity for bills and essential variable costs.
- **Emergency Reserve:** multi-month essential-expense coverage.
- **Vehicle Reserve:** ownership-cost and replacement planning, including insurance, fuel, maintenance, registration, and parking/tolls.
- **Annual-obligation reserve:** known irregular obligations that are not ordinary monthly spending.
- **Medical and other reserves:** policy-designated gaps that must be funded before opportunity or investment capital.

Reserve gaps are explicit deductions in the Capital Governor input snapshot. A gap is not subtracted again through a forecast that already includes the same obligation.
