# P&L Period Integrity

P&L reports expose their start and end period and remain review-gated until their line items and reported totals reconcile.

Overlapping cumulative reports are not added together. For example, a January–June report and a January–July report are alternate coverage views, not additive monthly income. Same-period reports require explicit duplicate/version review before one can supersede the other.

Settlement coverage and P&L coverage are reconciled as separate evidence views. A period mismatch produces a review state rather than forced equality. A P&L does not create a settlement, owner draw, finance transaction, or verified household income.
